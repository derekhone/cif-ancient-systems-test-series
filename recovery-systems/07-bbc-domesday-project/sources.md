# BBC Domesday Project — Sources

This document lists the primary and secondary sources cited in the case study.

---

## Primary Sources — Domesday Recovery Project

- Darlington, Jeffrey; Finney, Andy; & Pearson, Andy (2003). "Domesday Redux: The Rescue of the BBC Domesday Project." *RLG DigiNews* 7(5).
  - First-hand account of the emulation project and recovery process.

- UK National Archives & University of Leeds (2004). *The Domesday Book Online*.
  - Web-based interface to the recovered BBC Domesday Project data.

- BBC (2003). "Domesday Reloaded." *BBC News*, December 2, 2003.
  - News coverage of the successful emulation and recovery.

---

## Digital Preservation and Emulation

- Ross, Seamus & Gow, Ann (1999). *Digital Archaeology: Rescuing Neglected and Damaged Data Resources*. British Library Research and Innovation Report 110. London: British Library.
  - Early digital preservation research; includes Domesday case study.

- Ross, Seamus (2000). "Changing Trains at Wigan: Digital Preservation and the Future of Scholarship." *NPO Preservation Guidance: Occasional Papers*. London: National Preservation Office.
  - Broader context for digital preservation challenges.

- Holdsworth, David & Wheatley, Paul (2001). "Emulation, Preservation, and Abstraction." *RLG DigiNews* 5(4).
  - Technical discussion of emulation as a preservation strategy.

---

## LaserDisc Technology

- BBC & Acorn Computers (1986). *Domesday Project Technical Specifications*. Internal documentation.
  - Original technical specs for the BBC Domesday Project hardware and software.

- Philips Electronics (1980s). *LaserVision Technical Manual*.
  - LaserDisc player specifications and data encoding.

---

## The Original Domesday Book (1086)

- UK National Archives. *Domesday Book* (1086). London: The National Archives.
  - Original medieval manuscript; available for viewing in the National Archives.

- Hallam, Elizabeth M., ed. (1986). *Domesday Book Through Nine Centuries*. London: Thames and Hudson.
  - Historical analysis of the original Domesday Book and its preservation.

- Roffe, David (2000). *Domesday: The Inquest and the Book*. Oxford University Press.
  - Scholarly study of the creation and purpose of the 1086 Domesday Book.

---

## Digital Preservation Theory and Practice

- Rothenberg, Jeff (1999). "Avoiding Technological Quicksand: Finding a Viable Technical Foundation for Digital Preservation." Council on Library and Information Resources.
  - Seminal paper on digital preservation challenges; advocates for emulation.

- Lynch, Clifford (1999). "Canonicalization: A Fundamental Tool to Facilitate Preservation and Management of Digital Information." *D-Lib Magazine* 5(9).
  - Framework for managing format diversity and obsolescence.

- Lavoie, Brian (2014). "The Open Archival Information System (OAIS) Reference Model: Introductory Guide," 2nd ed. Digital Preservation Coalition.
  - Standard model for digital archiving and long-term preservation.

---

## Format Obsolescence and Migration

- Lawrence, Gregory W., et al. (2000). "Risk Management of Digital Information: A File Format Investigation." Council on Library and Information Resources.
  - Analysis of file format longevity and obsolescence risk.

- Digital Preservation Coalition (2008). *Preservation Management of Digital Materials: The Handbook*. York, UK: Digital Preservation Coalition.
  - Comprehensive guide to digital preservation strategies.

---

## Analog vs. Digital Longevity

- Brand, Stewart (1999). *The Clock of the Long Now: Time and Responsibility*. New York: Basic Books.
  - Philosophical and practical discussion of long-term thinking; includes digital vs. analog preservation.

- Gladney, H.M. (2007). *Preserving Digital Information*. Berlin: Springer.
  - Comprehensive technical and policy discussion of digital preservation.

---

## CIF and Technological Context

- Conway, Paul (2000). "Overview: Rationale for Digitization and Preservation." In *Handbook for Digital Projects: A Management Tool for Preservation and Access*. Andover, MA: Northeast Document Conservation Center.
  - Context for digitization as both access and preservation strategy.

- Thibodeau, Kenneth (2002). "Overview of Technological Approaches to Digital Preservation and Challenges in Coming Years." In *The State of Digital Preservation: An International Perspective*. Council on Library and Information Resources.
  - Comparative analysis of migration, emulation, and other preservation strategies.

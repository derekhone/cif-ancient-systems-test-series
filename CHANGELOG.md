## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Restructured the repository from a single-paper repo into the **CIF Ancient Systems Test Series**.
- `framework/CIF_Framework.md` — full description of the Coherent Inheritance Framework: status and provenance (including the UIP → CIF renaming), central proposition, six axioms, five pillars, the canonical case-study structure, and the rationale for testing against ancient systems.
- Case study #02 — **Aboriginal Oral Traditions** (`case-studies/02-aboriginal-oral-traditions/`), with sources.
- Case study #05 — **Japanese Tsunami Stones** (`case-studies/05-tsunami-stones/`), with sources.
- `assets/` — Giza concept imagery.
- Placeholder scaffolding for case studies #03, #04, #06, #07, #08 and the series `synthesis/`.
- A shared **Final CIF Verdict** taxonomy (`SUPPORTED` / `PARTIALLY SUPPORTED` / `INCONCLUSIVE` / `CHALLENGED` / `FALSIFIED AS FRAMED`) and a mandatory **Falsification Check** in every case study.

- Updated `CITATION.cff` to reflect the series title.
